# Sobrado do Alpendre

A preliminary architectural study, in Portuguese, of a three story house of about 306 m² on a 10 × 20 m lot in Brasília, with interactive plans, sections and facades, a sun path chart, a daylight map and a 3D model.

Live: https://oliveira-teixeira.github.io/design-sobrado-do-alpendre/

## Stack

- HTML, CSS and JavaScript; plans, sections and facades drawn as inline SVG
- three.js r186, bundled as ES modules in `3d.js` (the 3D model page, `3d.html`) and `luz.js` (the daylight map, loaded on first use)
- Google Fonts: Fraunces, Inter, JetBrains Mono
- 1k PBR texture maps for wood planks, clay roof tiles and white plaster in `assets/`

Exported from a claude.ai artifact on 9 Oct 2026.
